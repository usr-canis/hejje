# Hejje Guruthu – Privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Hejje Guruthu ("the app") records your drives on your phone. This policy explains what the app
does with your information. In short: **your trips stay on your phone**. There are no accounts,
no cloud storage, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What the app stores, on your phone only

- **Location while a drive is recorded**: GPS positions, speed and altitude, used to draw your
  route and work out distance, speed and other trip statistics.
- **Motion sensor readings** (accelerometer, gyroscope, gravity, barometer) while a drive is
  recorded, used to measure speed, braking, acceleration and turns more accurately.
- **Your car's Bluetooth name and address**, which you choose in Settings, so recording can start
  and stop when your phone connects to and disconnects from the car.
- **Places you name** (for example home, work, or a food stop), their ratings, and notes and tags
  you add to trips.
- **Where you last parked.**
- **Settings** and a short **activity log** of recording events, to help diagnose problems.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. The developer never receives
it. It is not shared with anyone unless you choose to share or export it yourself.

## Location in the background

The app can record drives automatically when your phone connects to your car, even when the app is
closed. For this it asks for location access "all the time". Location is used only while a drive
is being recorded, and only for the purposes above. A notification is shown whenever recording is
in progress. You can turn automatic recording off in Settings, or remove the permission in Android
settings at any time.

## What leaves your phone

The app uses the internet for these things only:

1. **Map tiles** from [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org), to draw maps. Like any web request,
   this tells the tile server your IP address and which map area is being shown.
2. **Place names** from the [Nominatim](https://nominatim.org) service of OpenStreetMap, to name
   where a trip started and ended. The app sends the coordinates of those points. This can be
   switched off in Settings ("Look up place names").
3. **Replay videos**, only when you tap "Make replay video" on a trip. The video is made on your
   phone. To draw it, the app downloads satellite imagery of the route's area from
   [EOX](https://s2maps.eu) and map tiles from OpenFreeMap, and asks
   [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) for that trip's weather: it sends the date and the car's
   rough position each hour, rounded to about 10 km. The finished video stays on your phone
   until you share or save it. Private places are cut out of the video's route, as in share cards.

These services have their own privacy policies:
[OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/privacy/),
[OpenStreetMap Foundation](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy),
[EOX](https://eox.at/privacy-policy/) and [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms#privacy).

Nothing else is sent anywhere. The app does not contain analytics, crash-reporting or advertising
code.

## Android Auto

On Android Auto the app shows your current drive and your saved stops on the car's screen. This
uses the same information stored on your phone; nothing extra is collected.

## Sharing, export and backup

You decide when to share a trip picture, export a GPX file or make a backup. Shared and exported
files go only where you send them. Automatic backups are written to a folder you choose.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Delete all data** removes all trips, places and settings from the app.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
